# Forge Studio — user stories

> The complete list of operator journeys Forge Studio purports to support, tiered against the 1.0 gate. This file is the product's story catalogue: `docs/roadmaps/1.0.md` §3 takes its gate list from here, the story harness (`tests/stories/`) implements the G-tier rows, and the M4 cull ledgers take their cull candidates from §8. When a story changes, change it here first.
>
> Derived 2026-08-28 from two independent drafts (product surface: MVUS, operator journey, 21 journeys, 33 routes, bridge route groups · intent/market: roadmaps R3/R4/R6, ADRs 027–045, the software-factory vocabulary) merged and re-verified against main `03bf47db`.

**Actor.** One technical operator running a portfolio of side projects, who never leaves Studio. Vocabulary: **Factory** (one assembled, running pipeline; the OOTB develop flow is one) · **Flow** (ordered stations) · **Station** (where an agent or gate acts) · **Gate** (human or automated approval) · **Agent** · **Skill** · **Brain** (in-product) / Knowledge (outward).

**Tier key.** **G** = 1.0 gate (a story with beats, a script, a clip and a usage-doc fragment) · **1** = must work at 1.0, proven by a named story beat or a package contract test · **2** = must work at 1.0 as its own story (promoted to S10) · **3** = post-1.0 · **⚑** = a surface no operator asked for — cull candidate.

**Groups.** BUILD a factory (B) · RUN a factory (R) · TUNE the factory (T) · OPERATE the platform (O). Row count: 9 G + 10 B + 14 R + 5 T + 7 O = **45**, plus S0 (harness smoke) and S10 (promoted from R-01).

## 10. Maintenance rule

- A new operator capability is not real until it has a row here with a tier, and a G-tier row is not real until `tests/stories/<id>.story.mjs` exists and runs.
- A surface with no row is a cull candidate by definition; add the row or remove the surface.
- Tier changes are recorded with the date and the evidence (a story beat, a contract test, an incident).
- Post-1.0 rows (tier 3) are not roadmap commitments; they are the register that keeps the platform honest about what a factory construction platform is expected to do.
