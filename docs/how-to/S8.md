## 5. Check what every declared source contributes — and what the ones contributing nothing say

This is what "browse the registry" has to mean, and it has three honest endings rather than one. A source forge can reach contributes what it publishes — the MCP registry proposes rows here, and says how much of itself it was read. A source forge cannot reach says so on its own chip: skills.sh sits outside the fetch allowlist, because letting forge call a new origin is a decision about dependencies, not a refresh. And a source forge reads and finds nothing forge can install is a third thing again — claude-code-templates publishes hooks, and install-by-URL knows only skill packages. What none of these does is write to your registry: discovery PROPOSES, and a human promotes. A list that grew by itself would be a list you did not choose.
