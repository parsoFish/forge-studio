Decision points on this page

1 Kickoff context density — the full contract card (agent, stages, artifact, brain access, tools, interaction shape) vs just the kind title + one line. Anything here you'd never read? Anything missing (budgets? cost ceiling)?

2 Tier picker presentation — radio cards with speed/cost hints vs a compact segmented control; out-of-range tier shown disabled with the "outside skill range" tooltip vs hidden entirely. (Switch to architect or authoring to see different ranges.)

3 Fixed-strategy chip — switch to demo, project-brain, onboarding or kb-cleanup: no picker, a read-only chip. Is the chip worth the row, or should fixed kinds drop the model section?
